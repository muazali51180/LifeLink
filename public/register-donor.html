Register as a Volunteer Blood Donor

Every donation can save up to 3 lives. Register your details so patients and hospitals in your city can connect with you during urgent emergencies.

🩺 Donor Eligibility Guidelines

  • Age: Must be between 18 and 60 years old.
  • Weight: Minimum weight of 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • Donation Interval: At least 3 months (90 days) since your last blood donation.
  • Health Status: No history of active blood-borne viral infections (Hepatitis B/C, HIV).
  • Screening: Hospital blood banks always perform cross-matching and hemoglobin screening before draw.

🔒 Privacy & Security

Your information is used solely for legitimate medical blood search requests. You can update your availability status or remove your profile at any time.
