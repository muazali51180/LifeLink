How LifeLink Works

A fast, 3-step bridge between patients and volunteer donors:

1

Describe Your Need

Type naturally in the search box, for example: "Need 2 units of O+ blood in Lahore right now".

2

Clinical Compatibility Match

The matching engine identifies both exact matches and compatible donor types (such as O- universal donors).

3

Direct Contact (Call/WhatsApp)

Navigate to matched donors and instantly dial or send a pre-filled WhatsApp message to arrange donation.

⚠️
Important Medical & Emergency Notice:

LifeLink is a prototype for Hacktoberfest Lahore 2026. It facilitates peer-to-peer connection with volunteer blood donors. It does not replace 1122 or hospital emergency blood banks. Always perform proper laboratory cross-matching, screening, and consult certified medical doctors before transfusion.
