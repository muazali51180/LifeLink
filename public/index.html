Our Purpose

Turning Desperate Searches Into Instant, Clinical Matches

Every single day across Pakistan, hospital wards and intensive care units experience critical blood shortages. During emergency surgeries, major road accident traumas, obstetric complications, or pediatric thalassemia transfusions, families often lose precious hours calling relatives, posting across social media, or forwarding urgent pleas into crowded chat groups.

LifeLink was created to solve this urgent human problem. By uniting an open-weight artificial intelligence engine (Meta LLaMA 3.1 8B on Groq) with certified clinical erythrocyte compatibility matrices, LifeLink eliminates guesswork and connects patient attendants to verified, willing donors in their city in under 60 seconds.

🧠 Open-Weight AI Intelligence

Patients write in everyday conversational English or Roman Urdu. The system accurately extracts the required blood group, hospital, and city without forcing users through tedious multi-step questionnaires.

🩺 Clinical Compatibility First

We don't just match identical blood labels. The platform validates against the medical ABO and Rh(D) red blood cell transfusion matrix—instantly surfacing universal O- donors when a rare blood type is urgently required.

⚡ 1-Click Direct Coordination

No phone number copy-pasting, no waiting for emails. Every matched donor profile provides direct phone dialing and pre-formatted emergency WhatsApp messaging ready to send in 1 tap.
